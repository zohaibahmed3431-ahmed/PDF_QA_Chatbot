Company Handbook

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Company laptops must use multi-factor authentication and full-disk encryption.

Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual leave days and 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year. Leave requests should normally be submitted at least three working days in advance.
