University Student Guide

Library Services
The main library is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Students may borrow up to six books for 14 days.

Academic Grading
The grading scale uses A, B, C, D, and F. A grade requires a final score of 85 percent or higher. Students need a minimum overall score of 50 percent to pass a course.
