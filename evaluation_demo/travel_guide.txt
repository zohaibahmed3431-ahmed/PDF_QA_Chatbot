City Travel Guide

Public Transport
The Green Line metro operates from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM. A standard one-way ticket costs 2.50 units. The central station connects the Green Line with the airport shuttle.

Museum Visit
The City History Museum is closed on Mondays. From Tuesday to Sunday it opens at 10:00 AM and closes at 6:00 PM. Student admission is 5 units.
